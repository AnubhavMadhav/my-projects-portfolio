This Site uses minimal local storage or cookies solely to enhance the User experience.
* **Functional Cookies:** The Site may use local storage to remember your **Light/Dark Mode** preference so you don't have to toggle it on every visit.
* **No Tracking:** I do not use third-party advertising cookies or invasive tracking pixels.

#### How we protect your information
I adopt appropriate data collection, storage, and processing practices and security measures to protect against unauthorized access. The Site is served over a secure SSL connection (HTTPS).
