## The Concept
"Just A Rather Very Intelligent System" (J.A.R.V.I.S) was a group experiment to merge **Web-based Augmented Reality** with **Voice AI**. We wanted to control the web not just with clicks, but with voice commands and visual AR markers.

## The Solution
We built a web application where users could scan a specific "Hiro Marker" to activate a 3D holographic interface.

- **Tech Stack:** **Django** (Backend), **AR.js** & **Three.js** (3D Rendering), **Web Speech API** (Voice Recognition).
- **Capabilities:**
    - **Voice Automation:** "Send email to [Name]" or "Play music" triggers backend automation scripts.
    - **AR Visualization:** Renders 3D models overlaying the real world via the webcam.

### Key Features
- **Hands-Free Control:** Full voice navigation for sending emails and WhatsApp messages.
- **3D Interactive UI:** Replaced standard buttons with floating 3D AR elements.
